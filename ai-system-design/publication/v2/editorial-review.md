## What changed for the reader

V1 begins inside a classification-status problem. V2 establishes a GitHub organization task and explains the local product before introducing taxonomy. Its first examples distinguish a public framework from an application belonging to a specific initiative, then carry those meanings through both AI phases.

The article retains the earlier insights and adds inspectable data rather than meeting a shortening quota. The full request artifacts support the explanation; they do not replace the article's examples and mechanisms.

## Editorial rubric

Scale: 1 = absent/obstructive; 3 = understandable with material gaps; 5 = clear throughout; 2/4 are intermediate judgments. These ratings are author assessments, not measured reader responses.

| Dimension | V1 | V2 | Evidence for the assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Premise/relevance | 2 | 4 | V2 identifies the task, product and useful outcome in the opening; v1 opens with undefined internal status vocabulary. |
| Narrative continuity | 3 | 4 | V2 carries purpose and initiative membership through discovery, retrieval, classification and repeat work. Counterexamples explain the limits of the preceding mechanisms. |
| Explanation/depth | 3 | 5 | Actual evidence, phrase weights, six candidates, payload/schema, observed selections and lifecycle states are inspectable. Prior insights remain covered. |
| Evidence/calibration | 5 | 5 | Existing result qualifications are retained; new traces match logical request keys, and hypothetical/observed/proposed material is labeled. |
| Readability/voice | 3 | 4 | Terms arrive after their purpose, examples carry the argument and the ending gives a usable tracing exercise. The longer technical read still requires attention. |
| Visuals/practical payoff | 3 | 4 | Two focused new illustrations join the established figures; captions explain provenance and the reader receives an explicit exercise. Small in-image labels require enlargement on phones. |

No score claims that the rewrite will produce a particular click-through rate, completion rate or social reach. Independent reader feedback and post-publication analytics remain unavailable.

## Remaining limits and handoff

- Current serialized bodies are offline reconstructions, not historical wire captures, despite matching logical cache identity and dependency versions.
- The proposed Jev integration has no task-specific measurements. The cited recent preprints were reviewed at abstract/metadata level, not fully audited or replicated.
- Full independent recomputation of the historical experiment depends on preserved local inputs. Exported data makes the selected traces inspectable without implying that the entire benchmark is publicly reproducible.
- The article intentionally retains a long-form technical explanation. Publishing notes provide navigation and format adaptations rather than a shortened replacement.
- Independent human comprehension/engagement evaluation is still a future activity. The user's review of this edition can provide actual feedback.

Deterministic packaging, source/preservation, data and rendering results are recorded in [verification.json](verification.json). These checks support factual and artifact integrity; they do not turn editorial judgment into an empirical readership study.
