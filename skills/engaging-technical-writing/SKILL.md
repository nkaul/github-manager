# Engaging Technical Writing

Help a reader understand a useful problem, follow the reasoning and inspect the evidence. Engagement should come from relevance, concrete detail and an explanation that keeps moving.

## Choose the work that is needed

- For drafting or a structural rewrite, read [narrative and explanation](references/narrative-and-explanation.md).
- For diagnosis or refinement, read [review and refinement](references/review-and-refinement.md).
- For publication handoff, read [platform adaptations](references/platform-adaptations.md) after the main argument is stable.
- For adaptation history and licensing, see [provenance](references/provenance.md). Routine drafting does not require it.

A narrow edit needs only the relevant guidance. Do not expand it into an unsolicited article rewrite.

## Essential working contract

1. Establish the audience, their task, the friction, the useful outcome and the article's central claim. Infer these from available context when possible.
2. Inventory substantive insights and evidence. When revising, map them into the new draft so improved readability does not silently remove content. Follow the user's preferences on depth and length.
3. Establish the problem before relying on product vocabulary. Show a concrete example before an unfamiliar abstraction; explain observable behavior before implementation details.
4. Build a connected argument. Each section should resolve a need established by preceding material. Choose a narrative shape that fits the evidence; do not invent failures to fill a template.
5. Make mechanisms inspectable: input → operation → output → consequence. For AI pipelines, show data crossing model boundaries and distinguish observed results, deterministic reconstructions, authored examples and proposals.
6. Refine structure, explanation, then prose. For a substantial rewrite, use up to three focused refinement passes and record material issues and changes. Report remaining weaknesses honestly.

Preserve uncertainty where evidence requires it and keep material qualifications close to their claims. Do not invent experiences, customer stories, credentials, performance gains, citations, reader studies or hidden model reasoning. A figure reinforces an explanation; the prose must work without it.

Match the author's voice instead of imposing first person, a persona or another writer's cadence. Readability statistics and editorial scores are diagnostic aids, not proof of comprehension or future engagement. End with a useful consequence or next action connected to the opening problem. Drafting does not authorize external publication.
