Establish the premise before introducing product terminology. Build a connected
explanation from concrete examples. Show input, operation, output and consequence,
including actual retrieval, augmentation and model input when relevant. Separate
measurements, reconstructions and proposals. Improve clarity without dropping
substantive insights. Review structure, explanation and prose, then record the
material changes and remaining weaknesses. Preserve prior versions.
