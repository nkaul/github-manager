# Platform adaptations

Load after the article's argument and evidence are stable. Verify current platform limits or behavior only when the task depends on them; do not assume ranking algorithms or ideal posting schedules.

## Medium, Substack and LinkedIn articles

Prepare a specific headline, explanatory subtitle and preview that establish the reader's problem and payoff. Preserve the full explanation and material qualifications. Use meaningful headings and orienting passages to support a longer read.

Keep Markdown image placement, caption and alt text together. Editors may require image uploads. Document the intended order and offer an equivalent list if a table does not render, retaining its numbers and conditions.

## Newsletter introduction

Write a short invitation grounded in the reader's task. Explain the relevance without invented urgency. Lead into the article without duplicating its opening paragraphs.

## LinkedIn feed

Make the post stand alone with its own premise, one concrete example and a useful implication. Do not compress every section into slogans. Preserve essential scope beside any metric, or omit the metric if its qualification cannot fit. Keep the author's voice and avoid fabricated experiences, suspense or engagement bait. Record character count as handoff information, not proof of current platform acceptance.

## Handoff

Provide selected and alternative headlines, subtitle, preview, platform copy, image placements/alt text and editor-facing evidence notes. Do not invent a byline or credentials. Separate publishable prose from verification artifacts. Local drafting does not authorize posting.
