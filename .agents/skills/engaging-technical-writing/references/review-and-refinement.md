# Review and refinement

Scale the workflow to the requested change. A small edit does not need every pass.

## Factual gates

Material claims need sources, inspectable calculations or clear labels as interpretation. Preserve measurement conditions, denominators and limits. Distinguish implementation, historical observation and proposal. Verify examples and model inputs against their artifacts. Preserve user-required content and prior versions. Check links, image paths, captions and diagram behavior.

An unsupported claim needs investigation, qualification or removal. Never manufacture a source or a better result. A style score cannot excuse a factual failure.

## Focused passes

1. **Structure:** read the opening, headings and ending together. Can a first-time reader identify the problem and solution? Reverse-outline the actual paragraphs and repair missing premises, tangents and transitions.
2. **Explanation:** follow the principal example. Identify each input, operation, output and consequence. Repair unexplained terms and reasoning gaps while preserving the source's important distinctions.
3. **Prose and presentation:** read aloud or perform an explicit sentence-level read. Fix cumbersome syntax, unclear pronouns, jargon and unhelpful repetition. Check captions, alt text and narrow-screen legibility.

For a substantial rewrite, make up to three focused refinement passes unless new evidence warrants more. Record issue → change → observed improvement. If a material gap remains, report it instead of declaring a complete pass.

## Rubric with evidence

Assess premise/relevance; narrative continuity; explanation/depth; evidence/calibration; readability/voice; visuals/practical payoff. If scores help, use 1–5 with passage-level observations: 1 = absent or obstructive; 3 = understandable with material gaps; 5 = clear throughout; 2 and 4 = intermediate judgments. A target of at least 4 per dimension can guide a substantial revision, but is not a universal publishing rule. Use the same criteria for before/after review and never average away a failed factual gate.

Cold-read checks: Can the intended reader explain whom this helps and why? Can they follow the worked example without filling in steps? Can they distinguish observations from reconstructions and proposals? What useful action or mental model do they leave with?

Self-review is not an independent human study. Label it accordingly. Actual engagement requires reader feedback or publication analytics; do not claim it from an editorial score.

## Deterministic checks and skill pilots

Use existing project tools when available. Word counts, long sentences, paragraph lengths and first-use terms locate possible problems; do not mechanically optimize to a grade or contraction quota. Preserve exact identifiers in data blocks. Check code or calculations only where the article relies on them.

For a new skill, static validation proves packaging only. Pilot a realistic premise revision and a fact-preserving technical explanation. Inspect whether the output establishes the problem and keeps the evidence intact. Record observed limitations and narrow fixes. Do not label self-generated test output as independent evaluation.
